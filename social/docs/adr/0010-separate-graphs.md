# ADR-0010: Separate Steam and platform graphs

Status: accepted design; future-phase behavior remains planned.

## Context

Observed external friendship does not express platform consent.

## Decision

Steam friend edges stay separate from accepted canonical SteamScope connections.

## Alternatives considered

Auto-connect imports; a shared relationship table.

## Consequences

Explicit accept flow and separate retention/discovery rules.

## Revisit conditions

Product changes still require affirmative consent and a migration design.
